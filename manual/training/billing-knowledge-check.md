# Billing knowledge check — for the Training module

Removed from the procedures manual (was §10-A) in Phase 3 of the 2026-10 manual update. Decision 56 (operator, 2026-10-06): the knowledge check belongs in Team Tools' **Training** module as a logged quiz, not in the manual. An unlogged in-manual version is a later, optional item.

To use it: create a quiz in the app (Training → Team Training) with these ten questions; the answer key is at the end. The "See" column uses the manual's current numbering — re-check it after the Phase 4 renumber.

This file is not part of the manual build.


Ten questions covering the rules most often answered wrongly. Each has one best answer; the key gives the reasoning and the section to revisit. Retained from Billing v2.0 Appendix A.

**1. A patient's oxygen concentrator rental has reached month 37. Which statement is correct?**

- A. The patient now owns the concentrator and UMS has no further obligation
- B. UMS keeps ownership, stops billing rental, and continues to service the equipment through the 5-year useful life
- C. The rental restarts for another 36 months
- D. The concentrator must be picked up immediately

**2. What happens after the 13th paid month of a Medicare capped rental hospital bed?**

- A. UMS offers the patient a purchase option; if they decline, rental continues to month 15
- B. Ownership transfers to the patient automatically and Medicare stops paying rental
- C. The bed must be returned and a new rental begins
- D. Medicare continues paying 7.5% per month indefinitely

**3. A patient calls saying they have a QMB card and received an invoice for their 20% coinsurance on a wheelchair rental. What should you do?**

- A. Collect the payment; QMB only covers premiums
- B. Offer a partial-payment plan
- C. Explain that QMB patients cannot be billed for Medicare cost-sharing, don't collect, and route to Billing to correct and refund
- D. Tell the patient to call Medicaid to pay the bill

**4. A patient has Original Medicare primary and Oklahoma Medicaid secondary. UMS is not enrolled with Oklahoma Medicaid. They're ordering a heavy-duty rollator. Is the heavy-duty upgrade fee charged?**

- A. Yes — UMS isn't enrolled in Oklahoma, so no Medicaid waiver applies
- B. No — any active Medicaid plan in any state waives both rollator fees (Rule A)
- C. Only the standard rollator fee is waived; the heavy-duty fee is charged
- D. Yes, but at the Field Ops rate

**5. A patient with BCBS commercial insurance as primary orders a fully-electric hospital bed and a standard rollator. Which upgrade fees apply?**

- A. Both fees are waived
- B. Both fees are charged
- C. The bed fee is waived; the rollator fee is charged
- D. The rollator fee is waived; the bed fee is charged

**6. For Medicare to continue covering a CPAP after the 90-day trial, the patient must:**

- A. Use it at least 4 hours a night on at least 70% of nights in a consecutive 30-day period, **and** have a face-to-face follow-up with their doctor between day 31 and day 91
- B. Use it every night for 90 days
- C. Have their doctor send updated paperwork every 6 months
- D. Sign an ABN

**7. A hospital bed rental patient (month 6) is admitted to hospital for two weeks spanning the monthly billing date. What happens to the rental?**

- A. The rental is cancelled and the bed is picked up
- B. Month 6 is billed as usual
- C. Month 6 isn't billed while the patient is inpatient; the count pauses and resumes after discharge
- D. The 13-month clock restarts at month 1 after discharge

**8. A patient says their insurance sent a statement showing UMS "charged $900" for a walker, and they're upset. The best response is:**

- A. Apologize and offer a discount
- B. Explain that the statement is an EOB, not a bill; the billed charge is a standard submitted amount, and any bill from UMS is based only on the patient-responsibility line
- C. Tell them the price is set by Medicare
- D. Transfer to Field Ops

**9. A claim denies as "other insurance primary / COB information needed." The patient says they only have Medicare. Who can fix this?**

- A. UMS Billing can update Medicare's COB record
- B. The patient must call their insurer (1-800-MEDICARE for Original Medicare) to update their coordination of benefits; then Billing resubmits
- C. The doctor's office must send a new referral
- D. Nobody; the claim is final

**10. A family reports that a patient renting a hospital bed from UMS (month 5) has just elected hospice, and the bed relates to the terminal diagnosis. Which is correct?**

- A. Medicare continues paying UMS because the bed was ordered before hospice
- B. Medicare now pays the hospice agency for related equipment, so UMS rental claims will deny and a pick-up is likely unless the patient already owns it
- C. The family must pay out of pocket for the remaining 8 months
- D. The hospice agency will reimburse UMS directly

### Answer key

| # | Answer | Why | See |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **B** | Title stays with the supplier after the 36-month cap; servicing continues through the 5-year useful life | [[§10-13.2]] |
| 2 | **B** | Ownership transfers automatically after 13 months; the purchase-option and 15-month rules are obsolete | [[§10-13.1]] |
| 3 | **C** | Federal QMB billing protection — correct and refund | [[§10-6.3]] |
| 4 | **B** | Rule A: any Medicaid plan, any state, waives both rollator fees. Only QMB-only is limited to the standard fee | [[§10-16]] |
| 5 | **C** | Rule C waives hospital bed fees only | [[§10-16]] |
| 6 | **A** | Both the usage threshold and the follow-up visit are required | [[§3-7.4]] |
| 7 | **C** | A rental month isn't billable while the patient is inpatient on the billing date | [[§10-15]] |
| 8 | **B** | An EOB or MSN is not a bill; the allowed and patient-responsibility amounts are what matter | [[§10-18.2]] |
| 9 | **B** | Only the patient can update a payer's COB record | [[§10-7.1]] |
| 10 | **B** | The hospice per diem covers related equipment; a capped rental hasn't transferred ownership at month 5 | [[§10-12]] |
