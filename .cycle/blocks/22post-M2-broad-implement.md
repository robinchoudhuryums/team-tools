REGRESSION RISKS:
- A search that is exactly a section number ("1.5", "10.2") now jumps instead of text-searching when that section exists; a rep looking for a decimal quantity gets the jump row only.
- Manual sections cannot be fixed in the app (by design — the operator maintains the source). A typo goes live until the next export + import; "Suggest an edit" captures it.
- The manual's parts start collapsed in the tree, so a rep who browses rather than searches sees fourteen part names first; the router entry and number search sit above them.
- A part renders all its sections at once (Part 10 is the largest, ~26 sections); measured renderer cost is ~23 ms for the whole manual, but the payload of one getManualPart is the whole part's Markdown.
- The hover preview fires a getReferenceItem per distinct target (cached for the session); many hovers mid-call cost that many reads.
- The M1 articles.json is no longer what the export writes; an import of an OLD articles.json still works (a bare list), without meta.
