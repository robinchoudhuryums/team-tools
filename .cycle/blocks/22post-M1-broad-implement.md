REGRESSION RISKS:
- Any EXISTING article that used `>` now renders a quote instead of "&gt; …" text — a fix, but its look changes; a `>` typed at the start of a line (e.g. "> 5 lbs") now reads as a quote.
- Existing articles whose list items are indented two spaces now NEST, and an indented line under an item now joins it; flat lists render byte-identically (pinned).
- The import holds the ScriptLock while it writes. Creates are one batched write, but each UPDATE is a row write plus a revision append — a re-import touching ~160 articles could hold the lock long enough to delay punches; the dialog says to import outside busy hours.
- An update to a PUBLISHED article goes live immediately (status kept) — re-exporting the manual publishes its edits; a deliberate choice, stated in the dialog's result.
- The staggered ReviewedAt is back-dated: the review log shows a "review" date that no one performed. Deliberate (the operator's spread), and ReviewedBy is the publisher.
- A manual article deleted in the app is never re-created by an import (reported "deleted in the app"); restoring it means deleting its ledger row.
