REGRESSION RISKS:
(1) Trigger failure emails. A job that used to THROW out of its handler, such as the PTO credit's `waitLock` sitting before its try, is now caught and recorded as failed. Apps Script's own failure email no longer fires for it. The failure now reaches the health dot and the 9am digest instead: same audience, different channel.
(2) New stamps that used to be silent:
- urgent or weekly digests sent with an unreadable rep Sheet (partial);
- an enabled Timesheet archive with no tab (failed);
- a failed `checkOpenPunches` or a crashed self-test also stamp under their keys, though these are de-duplicated on the dot by `ownFinding`.
A deployment with a permanently unreadable rep Sheet will now show a standing amber line until that Sheet is fixed. That line is true.
(3) The nightly diagnostics purge now runs on every deployment. It takes the script lock briefly at 2am and writes a DiagnosticsPurge audit row each night. Its liveness row is now always checked, so a dead `runNightlyPurges` trigger becomes visible where it was not.
(4) A disabled job now CLEARS a stale failure. Before, DiagnosticsPurge, QaReviewPurge, TimesheetArchive and the PTO credit's "tracking off" path left an old stamp in place.
(5) The digests now do a few `appendRow` calls per run (one per manager). The urgent digest shares `runDailyChecks`' six minutes, and the cost is negligible at team size.
(6) The ManagerDailyBrief throw message lost its "the brief run failed: " prefix, because the runner records the bare error.
