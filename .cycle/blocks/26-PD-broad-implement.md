PD1 the reader | web-app/40_metrics.js, web-app/00_config.js | `getCdrPartialDays_` opens the CDR Report's `Partial Days` tab three columns wide (Date, Agent, Credit — `CDR_PARTIAL_COLS`) and only when row 1 names those three. REGRESSION RISKS: (1) The data stamp now depends on a fifth tab. If the Partial Days tab exists and cannot be read, there is no stamp and every call-data reader caches for five minutes until it can. A missing tab is not a failure. (2) At deploy every stamped key turns over once, so the first Dashboard and Stats loads read cold. (3) A Dashboard card served from its six-hour cache now needs the data stamp (one cache read, or a probe at most every two minutes across all users) and the kept marks; while the stamp cannot be read, the marks are read from the tab on every Dashboard call. (4) The two repos can disagree on an UNMARKED rep's days: the dashboard divides by days with calls, this app by workdays — as before this change. They agree on what a mark does. (5) If the dashboard names agents differently from the call data and no alias row exists, a rep's mark is ignored here; Storage Health counts such marks but, on a shared phone system, cannot tell a misnamed colleague from another department's agent. (6) A marked rep's month-to-date deltas on Answered and Missed change on the day this ships (they are now like for like); the delta's hover says "adjusted for partial days". (7) The team average on the two count trends changes slightly on any day a colleague was partial; nothing on screen says which day, by design.
