5.1

Scope and process

Power Mobility covers power wheelchairs (PWC) and power operated vehicles (POV, commonly called scooters). Manual wheelchairs are in 2.4.

A past PMD patient who wants a new PMD — five years after the last one, or sooner with a significant change in condition — starts with Sales, not Power: see 4.5.1.

5.1.1The Power process

Sales → Power (PAK → Qualifications → PAR) → Field Ops-Power

Phase What happens Sections
Sales and eligibilityInsurance confirmed, evaluation booked. Moves on at Eligible4.4
1 · Intake and education — PAKPPD interview, Medical Assistant Education, appointment. Ends when the F2F and PAK are received5.2
2 · QualificationsDocuments reviewed and requested — with PT and ATP evaluations alongside, if needed5.3 · 5.4 · 5.5
3 · Verification & authorizationOrder verification any time before ordering, then PAR packet prep and submission. Appeals if denied5.6 · 5.7
4 · Delivery (Field Ops Power)Manufacturer order, 2–3 weeks to the warehouse, scheduling, delivery5.8

An order enters Power once Sales confirms insurance compatibility and an appointment with a qualified provider is set. It leaves Power once authorization approval is received and it is pushed to Field Ops-Power.

5.1.2Timeline expectations

The national average turnaround for a PWC is about 90 days, and UMS is typically faster. Actual times vary widely — under 30 days to over a year — depending on provider responsiveness, insurance type, and patient circumstances.

Note
Using the 90 days.

Quoting the 90-day national turnaround alongside our faster average can help set accurate expectations for a caller who expects the order to be done in a few days. Then give the current stage and what it's waiting on — a patient told "90 days" at day 200 has been given a number, not an answer.

5.2

PAK (Pre-Appointment Kit)

Updated 10/07/2026

The PAK stage is about understanding the patient's mobility needs, preparing the packet of documents that is sent to the MDO before the upcoming Mobility Evaluation appointment, and making sure the MDO understands what needs to be done.

5.2.1PPD (Patient Provided Data)

The first stage of the Power process. Once eligibility is confirmed and an appointment set, Sales assigns the order to a PPD team member for a roughly 15-minute PPD Interview covering the patient's mobility issues, PMD options, and the process overall.

Figure pending — PPD note template — no image in the sanitized export

If the order clears this step, the team member creates the PAK documents and sends them to the MDO for the appointment date. The Medical Assistant Education team then contacts the MDO to confirm the PAK was received, and to discuss those documents with the doctor, Medical Assistant, or Nurse.

After the PPD, the PAK team sends the patient a link to an optional consent form and AOR form, used for an appeal if the order is later denied.

Watch-out
The Seating Evaluation mistake.

The PAK includes a pre-filled SE watermarked "Patient Provided Data" as a reference guideline (based on the patient's PPD responses and insurance guidelines), followed by two blank SE pages for the provider to complete from their own assessment. MDOs frequently return the pre-filled pages instead of completing the blank ones, so it is worth clarifying how that is meant to be completed.

5.2.2Medical Assistant Education

After the PAK documents are sent, the Medical Assistant Education team contacts the MDO's office — the doctor, medical assistant or nurse — about them — see 5.2.1.

When the Medical Assistant Education team is unavailable

Situation Action
Several days until the appointmentTell the MDO an associate will call back to go over the paperwork
Appointment is same-day, no Medical Assistant Education availableAdvise the MDO directly — see the four points below

For a same-day appointment, tell the MDO:

  1. Complete the paperwork per the written instructions during the visit
  2. The purpose of the visit must be a Power Mobility Device Evaluation
  3. SE pages watermarked "Patient Provided Data" are reference only — complete the blank copies
  4. Complete and return the documents, or give us an update as soon as they are able
  5. If the provider decides to refer the patient to a PT/OT for this, that's fine — but we'll then need a PT/OT Eval Rx for the insurance. We can help the patient through that process with whichever PT/OT they're referred to or already use, or help them find an eligible local service. See 5.4

5.2.3New appointment

Involved only when the patient needs to reschedule the Mobility Evaluation. Common reasons:

  • The original appointment wasn't specifically for a PMD Evaluation
  • The F2F is over 6 months old
  • The provider says they or the patient don't qualify

Ask whether the patient has made a new appointment. If yes, note the date, time, and provider. If no, explain why it's needed, and advise they schedule the appointment and call us back with the details. Contact one of the PAK Appointment Scheduling sub-team members — PAK Appointment Scheduling — if further assistance is needed.

5.2.4Appointment passed

When the Mobility Evaluation date has passed but the F2F and PAK documents haven't been returned, this sub-team contacts the MDO and the patient to confirm the appointment took place, and requests the pending paperwork if so.

Note

If the note reads "Trying to Obtain After Visit Documents," confirm the visit was completed specifically for a PMD Evaluation. We are most likely requesting the F2F and the completed PAK documents. Confirm the fax number and re-fax if needed.

Once the PAK documents and F2F are confirmed received, the order is pushed to Qualifications.

5.3

Qualifications

Once the F2F and PAK documents arrive, the Qualifications team reviews them and requests corrections or additional information per insurance guidelines. MDOs can often be unresponsive, so this team follows up regularly until all of the necessary documents are received.

5.3.1Status update calls

Most Power calls are status updates, and most of those are about documentation we've requested from the MDO.

Always check: open the Transaction tab and click Fax History, then open the most recent fax sent. Compare the documentation request against the most recent artifacts received, and explain any obvious discrepancy — a missing signature, the wrong provider, an incomplete form.

5.3.2Documentation request older than 3 days

In order:

  1. Confirm the fax number and ask for a backup number, then re-fax
  2. If the MDO is hard to reach, ask whether they can receive it by secure email — if so, you must enable Virtru protection
  3. Last resort: email the request to the patient, or have a Power team member physically mail the documents to the patient to bring to the MDO in person

5.3.3Qualified Lead (QL)

A patient who has already completed a valid Mobility Evaluation is a Qualified Lead. Once Sales Eligibility confirms eligibility, the order goes directly to T3Q (Qualifications sub-team), skipping the PAK stage. A PPD is still conducted — directly by a member of the Qualifications team rather than the PAK stage's PPD sub-team — and the order continues through Qualifications.

If the Mobility Evaluation is scheduled for a future date, the order follows the standard PAK process instead.
