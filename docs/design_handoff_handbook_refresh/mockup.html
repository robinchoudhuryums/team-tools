Out-of-pocket purchases

Read Oct 28Updated Oct 12·3 min

When a patient buys equipment outright, we aren't submitting to insurance, so no referral paperwork is required.

PolicyThe CSR team handles out-of-pocket purchases.Rather than transferring the caller, complete the purchase, then send one email to all three departments:
NotifyWhy
The intake team for the equipmentTo open the order
The eligibility teamSo no insurance verification is started
Field OperationsTo schedule delivery

Use the standard subject format from §0.11.2 — for example, OOP Purchase: Maria Garcia 456789.

ExceptionOxygen concentrators, PAP devices and ventilators need a prescription even when bought out of pocket.
