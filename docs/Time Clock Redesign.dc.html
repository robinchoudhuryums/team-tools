Dashboard Redesign

You describe this page as a dashboard for a rep's own and team metrics, their pending tasks, and clocking in and out. Two of those three are well served. Pending tasks aren't served at all — Requests and Training sit in a row labelled "extras", and the things a rep is actually behind on (an unacknowledged coaching note, a QA review, missing call notes) never reach this page.

So this isn't a rebuild. The metrics carousels stay as they are, per your call. What changes: a Needs you block leads the main column, the clock card gets denser so the punch buttons rise above the fold, and three duplicated readouts collapse to one each.

01 — The gap

Three jobs, two of them staffed

Own + team metrics
Well served. Two carousels × three periods, with deltas, banding, projections and an honest "comparison unavailable". Left alone.
Clock in / out
Works, but sits fifth in the rail under a tall decorative card. Not moved off the rail — just given room by tightening what's above it.
Pending tasks
Not staffed. Two of five sources appear, in a row called "extras". Coaching, QA and missing notes are absent — a rep can be behind on all three and this page looks clean.
Also collapsing
  • Hours worked renders twice, ~100px apart — the greeting sentence and the shift-strip header carry the same "5h 54m worked · 32m lunch". The #5a comment says hours moved to the strip; the sentence was never trimmed to match.
  • Breaks render three times — #clk-next-break inside the clock card, the B1/Lunch/B2 chips under the ribbon, and the ribbon's own scheduled band.
  • Two animations compete on the hero — the cross-fading sky with its star field and randomized shooting star, and the world-clock strip rotating US regions. Neither changes a decision a CSR makes.
  • The state pill is far from the state. "On the clock" sits in the greeting bar; the clock card 200px below it doesn't say whether you're on shift.

03 — Implementation notes

What this costs to build

Free — client only
  • Rail order: clock card → renderActions → shift strip. One move in the area.innerHTML template.
  • buildStatusSentence_ drops the worked/lunch clause — the shift strip owns hours (finish the #5a move). Keep "since" and "until end of shift".
  • State chip moves into the clock card foot; the greeting keeps .dash-onclock.
  • #clk-next-break retires — clkBreakScheduleHtml_ marks taken breaks struck-through and the next one outlined. Keep clkNextBreak_ (the shell's remindersTick_ still needs it).
  • Undo becomes a labelled button with its live remaining time, not a bare ×. Same eligibility rule — keep the undoDiff >= 0 sentinel guard.
  • Clock card height drops ~70px: world-clock strip and next-break chip out, state line in.
The new piece
One getMyPendingTasks() aggregate, rep-callable, returning a flat sorted array of { kind, title, detail, dueIso, overdue, route }. Five sources, four already reachable:
  • Coaching — unacknowledged, from getMyCoaching
  • QA — unacknowledged reviews (the My Reviews ack from the QA handoff)
  • Notes — answered-minus-logged, already computed by loadCoverageStrip_; reuse fileMissingCalls_ and its CLK_NAV_HINT
  • Training — getMyTraining, today's extras card
  • Requests — pending punch-edit / PTO, today's extras card
Sort overdue first, then by due date. Render nothing when empty — an empty "Needs you" is worse than none. The extras row keeps Spanish Inbox and Requests; Training folds into Needs you.
The rotator — your call, implemented
Keep the rotation, but never rotate away from an active shift. In clkGreetRotStart_, hold on slide 0 whenever stats.state is working or lunch; resume when the rep is out or hasn't clocked in. The held state needs to be visible — otherwise a rep who saw a What's-new slide yesterday reads a frozen line as a stuck page. Hence the small Held chip beside the sentence, which becomes the affordance to step through updates manually. Same reduced-motion and COMPACT_MODE early-outs as today.
The sky card — reworked, not removed
The time-of-day gradient stays, night sub-phases included — it's the app's one piece of warmth and the IST case is a real one. What goes is the rotating world-clock strip: it animates continuously, sits beside a second animation, and a CSR working one timezone never acts on it. The tz select already covers deliberate lookups. The star field stays but loses clkShootMaybe_ — a randomized shooting star on a timer is motion with no information in it, and it's the one piece that can fire while a rep is reading the card. Freed height goes to the state line and to lifting the punch buttons.
Do not break these
  • The compact pop-out gate. clkLoadDashboard_ and clkLoadDashboardExtras_ early-out under COMPACT_MODE (cycle-8 M-12) — three RPCs per launch against a hidden column. getMyPendingTasks needs the same gate, or it reintroduces exactly that cost.
  • Pending ≠ empty. undefined renders a skeleton; null renders "couldn't load". Needs you must follow the same rule — a failed fetch showing "nothing pending" is the worst possible failure on this card.
  • SWR without the flash. Cache Needs you the way CLK_DASH does (60s freshness, paint-then-refresh) — clkRefreshState_ re-renders the whole view on every focus.
  • The undo sentinel. timeDiffSecondsClient returns -1 for malformed and past-window times, and -1 <= 300 is true — that's why eligibility needs the non-negative check. A live countdown label must not reintroduce it.
  • Fixed colours on the sky card. Its gradient is identical in both themes, so any text on it uses a literal, never --muted/--ink — the V-2 finding. The new state line is on that card and is bound by it.
Smaller things
  • clkUpdateBreak_ builds a chip as an inline-style string in JS. Now that the chip retires, the pattern goes with it — the break chips are already classed.
  • Needs you is a list: real <ul>/<li> with real links, count announced (aria-label="Needs you, 4 items"), overdue carried in words not colour.
  • The rail is 360px and the punch primary is now full-width — comfortably past 44px, and better on the phone layout than the current inline row.
  • Visual: test/visual/mock.js needs a getMyPendingTasks fixture, plus a zero-item variant — the empty state is the one that changes most and the one a populated fixture never shows. Same gap the QA and Coaching handoffs each hit.
