The daily report, reframed for "My Stats"

Your legacy Daily Call Transfer Report is a flat spreadsheet — every rep gets the same grid and reads across to find themselves. In My Stats the same CDR data is reframed agent-first: your numbers lead as a hero with rank + queue breakdown, then the department rollup (with the month-over-month + abandonment-goal coding from the legacy header), then the full team table with your row pinned and highlighted. Same source spreadsheet, same figures — just legible at a glance.

The reframe
  • You first. The agent's own row is pulled up into a hero with rank, team-avg context, and their transfer breakdown — no scanning the grid to find yourself.
  • Department rollup keeps the legacy header's figures + the CM/LM month-over-month and the abandonment goal coloring (green under 5%).
  • Full team table preserves every per-queue column, with your row pinned & highlighted and sortable headers.
  • Same source: the existing CDR Report spreadsheet — the My Stats date selector picks the report day.
Open question: agents currently get this whole report by email. In-app, should a rep see every rep's row (transparent, shown here) or only their own + department aggregates? Managers clearly see all — say the word and I'll add a rep-scoped variant.
