UMS Team Tools · Metrics — My Stats · Trends (MTD / YTD)

The same report, over time

When the window is a single day, My Stats shows the daily snapshot. Switch the preset to MTD or YTD and the same cards gain trend: each hero metric grows a sparkline + a delta vs the prior period, a transfer-% / abandonment line chart spans the range against the goal line, and the team table swaps single-day queue counts for period totals + a per-rep trend. One mental model, two depths.

Day vs trend — what flips
  • Hero metrics gain a sparkline + a delta vs the prior period (▲/▼), so "how am I trending" reads instantly.
  • Department swaps the single-day rollup for a transfer-% / abandonment line chart across the range, with the 5% goal line and a "days over goal" callout.
  • Team table shows period totals + a per-rep weekly trend sparkline and a vs-last-month delta, instead of single-day queue counts.
  • Per-queue columns from the daily view collapse — expandable per rep — since 11 columns × a month is noise.
  • Projection. On MTD/YTD the department chart extends past “today” with a dashed run-rate line + cone of uncertainty to a projected month/year-end figure (“on pace for 37.2%”), against the target line — a client-side calc on the same daily series, gated to ≥ a few days elapsed.
  • Same preset control: DAY keeps the snapshot; MTD / YTD switches to this trend layout.
Data: all series come from the same CDR Report spreadsheet — trends are just the daily rows aggregated by week/month rather than read for one date.
