UMS Team Tools · Time Clock redesign

Time Clock — Clock & Coverage

The live shift strip becomes the control surface — punch buttons, hours-worked, breaks, and a call-volume histogram all live on it. Lunch is color-coded, the big clock gets a time-of-day sky + timezone switch, Sick is gone, PTO collapses into the consolidated card row, and Coverage becomes a single week heatmap. Light + the app's dark mode.
