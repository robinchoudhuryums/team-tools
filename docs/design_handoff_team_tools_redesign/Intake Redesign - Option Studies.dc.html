Intake — visual & interaction redesign

The Intake module never got the Console-redesign treatment the rest of the app has. It runs on a stray navy brand color, rolls its own button system, and renders all 45 PPD questions — including every yes/no and all 10 pain regions — as identical free-text inputs. Below: the module rebuilt on the shared design tokens, with the PPD form explored as three input treatments.
